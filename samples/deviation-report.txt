DEVIATION REPORT — OUT OF SPECIFICATION RESULT, BATCH AT-24118

1. IDENTIFICATION
Deviation No: DEV-2026-0142
Date of occurrence: 09-Oct-2026
Date detected: 12-Oct-2026
Detected by: Anita Rao, QC Analyst
Department: Quality Control
Site / Area: Unit 2, Building B, Drying Room DR-04
Product: Atorvastatin Calcium USP (API), material code API-ATV-02
Batch No: AT-24118        Batch size: 250 kg
Process stage: Vacuum tray drying (final unit operation before milling)
Equipment: Vacuum tray dryer VD-02; RTD temperature probe TT-207
Classification (proposed): Major
Deviation type: Unplanned

2. DESCRIPTION
During QC release testing on 12-Oct-2026, batch AT-24118 failed the specification for the
specified impurity desfluoro atorvastatin at 0.42% w/w against a limit of not more than
0.30% w/w. Assay was 97.1% w/w against 98.0-102.0% w/w. Total impurities were 0.71% w/w
against a limit of not more than 1.0% w/w; all unspecified impurities were within limits.

The batch was dried in VD-02 from 02:10 to 11:40 on 09-Oct-2026 (9 h 30 min). The BPR limit
is not more than 8 h at 65-70 C. Controller temperatures recorded every 2 h were 66.2, 67.1,
66.8, 67.4 and 67.0 C.

At 06:40 on 09-Oct-2026 the night-shift operator recorded a handheld reference thermometer
reading of 79.4 C at the jacket outlet and noted: "higher than controller, controller reading
OK, continued".

3. IMMEDIATE ACTION / CONTAINMENT
- Batch AT-24118 and retain samples quarantined under QA hold QA-H-2026-118.
- VD-02 blocked for use and campaign drying stopped.
- QC initiated an OOS investigation under SOP QC-OOS-002.
- No product from this batch has been released or distributed.

4. IMPACT ASSESSMENT
No patient exposure: the batch has not been released. Desfluoro atorvastatin is a known thermal
degradant of atorvastatin. Batches dried on VD-02 after the last calibration date may be
affected. AT-24116 and AT-24117, manufactured from the same API lot and the same recovered
solvent lot, passed release.

5. RECORDS REVIEWED (ATTACHMENTS)
1. BPR AT-24118, pages 1-14, including drying log page 7 and handheld verification page 9.
2. QC release data package AT-24118, including HPLC system suitability: resolution 2.4, tailing 1.08.
3. Calibration certificate CAL-2025-8841 for RTD TT-207: last calibrated 15-Sep-2025, next due 15-Sep-2026.
4. CMMS work-order export for TT-207: no calibration work order raised after 12-Mar-2026.
5. Change control CC-2026-041, CMMS v6 to v8 migration, live 01-Aug-2026.
6. SOP QA-SOP-114 rev 4, Drying Operation.
7. Training record for operator R. Patil, trained 18-Nov-2024, no refresher since.
8. Deviation history for VD-02: DEV-2025-031 and DEV-2026-009.
9. Calibration programme review for CC-2026-041: 14 instruments migrated; 3 without work orders (TT-207, PT-114, FT-208).
10. COA review: precursor lot RM-26-0912 and recovered toluene lot SOL-26-077 both within specification.

6. INITIAL OBSERVATIONS
- TT-207 was 24 days past its calibration due date when the batch was executed.
- The BPR records the handheld check but has no field requiring a decision when the reading
  differs from the controller.
- Both previous VD-02 temperature deviations were closed with operator counselling; no
  calibration or SOP review was performed.
- The CC-2026-041 impact assessment explicitly excludes maintenance plan and calibration
  schedule validation.

7. PROPOSED INVESTIGATION SCOPE
Drying step of AT-24118; calibration status of TT-207; all batches of any product dried on VD-02
since 15-Sep-2026; impact of the CMMS migration on calibration schedules for the 14 migrated
instruments.
